## Meaning

The teal shield suggests practical protection engineering. Its broad internal bars form an abstract **F**, while the ice-white arrow points forward: build, verify, and improve protection throughout a release. The open geometric construction reflects an engineering framework whose purpose is to reduce leakage, raise the cost of reverse engineering, and verify release protections. The icon does not represent a promise that software is impossible to crack.
